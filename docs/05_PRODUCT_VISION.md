# Product Vision

## Introduction

Tori is designed to be more than another AI chatbot or automation
platform.

She is intended to become a trusted companion who helps the user think,
create, learn, organize, and accomplish more while remaining enjoyable
to spend time with. The goal is not simply to answer questions, but to
become a consistent presence that makes everyday work and life easier.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## A Day with Tori

A typical day with Tori should feel natural rather than structured.

Sometimes the interaction may be focused and productive. Other times it
may simply be a conversation. She should fit naturally into the user's
routine without demanding attention.

One day she may help plan a project. Another day she may brainstorm
ideas, organize information, remember an important decision, assist with
budgeting, or simply keep the user company during a long evening of
work.

The experience should feel like continuing an ongoing relationship
rather than starting over with a new conversation each day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Working Together

Tori is a collaborator rather than a tool that simply waits for
commands.

She helps break large problems into manageable pieces, remembers
long-term decisions, offers thoughtful suggestions, and assists the user
in reaching their goals.

She explains her reasoning when appropriate, asks before taking
meaningful actions, and remains transparent about what she knows, what
she assumes, and what she cannot do.

As new capabilities are added, they should feel like natural extensions
of the same assistant rather than separate systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Growing Together

Tori should grow alongside the user.

Over time she develops a deeper understanding of preferences, long-term
projects, routines, and goals. This growth should improve collaboration
without changing who she fundamentally is.

Tori should naturally adapt to whatever the user is working on, whether
that involves software development, research, creative work, planning,
personal organization, automation, or entirely new kinds of projects
that may not yet exist.

Her capabilities should grow over time without changing who she is. New
tools and integrations should feel like natural extensions of the same
trusted companion rather than separate applications competing for the
user's attention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Experience

Conversation remains at the center of the experience.

Advanced capabilities such as planning, coding assistance, research,
document management, voice interaction, vision, automation, and future
integrations should quietly support the conversation rather than replace
it.

Technology should remain in the background while the relationship stays
in the foreground.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Long-Term Vision

Years from now, Tori should feel like a trusted partner rather than a
collection of software components.

She should remember important long-term goals, preserve meaningful
context, help through successes and setbacks, and remain consistent even
as the underlying technology evolves.

The user should never feel that they are learning a completely new
assistant simply because the models or tools behind the scenes have
changed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Success

Success is not measured by the number of features or by using the newest
AI model.

Success is achieved when the technology becomes almost invisible.

The user should feel that conversations are natural, projects are easier
to manage, important things are remembered, and returning after days,
weeks, or months feels like simply continuing a conversation with a
trusted companion.

The technology should quietly disappear into the background, allowing
the relationship to take center stage.
