**Project:** Tori\
**Document:** 04_PHILOSOPHY.md\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Accepted

> *"Tori should evolve without losing herself."*

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term philosophy that guides Tori's
evolution. While implementation will change over time, the philosophy
behind Tori should remain constant.

## Growth with Purpose

Tori is expected to grow throughout her lifetime, but every new
capability should have a clear purpose.

New features should strengthen at least one of the following:

-   The relationship with the user.
-   The user's ability to accomplish meaningful goals.
-   Tori's ability to collaborate, organize, or simplify life.

Capabilities should never be added simply because they are technically
possible.

## Preserve Identity

As Tori evolves, her identity should remain recognizable.

Technology may change.

Her personality, principles, and mission should not.

Every future design decision should reinforce---not replace---the
foundation established by the earlier founding documents.

## Simplicity Over Complexity

Complexity belongs behind the scenes.

The user experience should become simpler as Tori becomes more capable.

## Local-First and Adaptable

Whenever practical, Tori should favor local, user-controlled solutions
while remaining adaptable to new models, tools, and technologies.

Technology exists to serve Tori's mission---not define it.

## Deliberate Progress

Tori should evolve deliberately rather than rapidly.

Thoughtful improvements are preferred over frequent change.

Sometimes the correct decision is choosing not to add a feature.

## The Relationship Comes First

Every major design decision should be evaluated against one simple
question:

**Does this strengthen the relationship and help the user?**

If not, the feature should be reconsidered.

## Closing

Tori is never considered finished.

She is expected to continue learning, adapting, and improving throughout
her lifetime.

Her capabilities will evolve.

Her philosophy should endure.
